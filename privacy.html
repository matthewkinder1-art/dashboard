Privacy Policy

This application is a personal, single-user tool operated by Matthew Kinder for his own use. It connects to third-party services (such as WHOOP) solely to retrieve his own account data.

What data is accessed

Health and fitness metrics from the account owner's own WHOOP account (recovery, sleep, strain, and related measurements), retrieved via the official WHOOP API with the account owner's explicit OAuth consent.

How data is used and stored

Data is retrieved for display on the account owner's private, password-protected personal dashboard and stored only on devices the account owner controls. Nothing is sold, shared, transferred, or disclosed to any third party. No advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind is involved.

Other users

This application has exactly one user — its operator. It is not offered to, and does not collect data from, anyone else.

Data deletion and contact

Access can be revoked at any time from the WHOOP app's integrations settings, which immediately ends all data retrieval. Questions: matthewkinder1@gmail.com.
